# Code of Conduct

## Scope

This code applies to Virgo Design issues, pull requests, discussions and other project-managed
community spaces. Everyone, including maintainers, is expected to follow it.

## Standards

- Treat others with respect. Technical disagreement and constructive criticism are welcome.
- Focus feedback on the work, acknowledge mistakes and respect personal boundaries.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, make personal attacks or share someone's private
  information without permission. Sexual harassment and unwanted sexual content are unacceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to [pokeya.mystic@gmail.com](mailto:pokeya.mystic@gmail.com).

Include relevant links and context. Do not publish sensitive information in an issue or discussion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, issue warnings, close or lock conversations, and
temporarily or permanently restrict participation in project spaces. They will consider the
severity, context and repetition of the behavior, explain decisions when appropriate, and respect
the privacy of those involved.
