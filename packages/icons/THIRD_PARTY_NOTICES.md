## Messi-inspired chest emblem

The fine chest emblem in `icons/shirt.svg` and `icons/shirt-filled.svg` is a
hand-drawn geometric outline inspired by Lionel Messi's personal mark.
Visual reference: [Messi personal mark](https://www.brandcrowd.com/blog/instagram-logos-from-the-top-20-influencers).
The Messi name and personal mark belong to their respective rights holders.

## GitHub mark

`icons/github.svg` and the generated `GithubIcon` use the geometry of
[Primer Octicons' mark-github-24](https://github.com/primer/octicons/blob/main/icons/mark-github-24.svg).
The SVG wrapper is adapted to Virgo's `currentColor` and consumer-controlled sizing conventions.

## Font Awesome brand marks

The `apple`, `windows`, `x-twitter` and `twitter` icon families adapt the
[Apple](https://fontawesome.com/icons/apple?style=brands),
[Windows](https://fontawesome.com/icons/windows?style=brands),
[X](https://fontawesome.com/icons/brands/solid/x-twitter) and
[Twitter](https://fontawesome.com/icons/brands/solid/twitter) icons from Font Awesome Free 7.3.1.
The paths were scaled to a 24-unit grid. The Apple and Windows paths were also adjusted for solid rendering.

[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## Linux penguin

The `linux` icon family is a simplified vector interpretation of [Tux](https://linuxfoundation.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/ES/pages/160202865/Can%2BI%2Buse%2BTux%2Bthe%2BPenguin),
the penguin originally drawn by Larry Ewing (lewing@isc.tamu.edu) with The GIMP.

## Japanese and Traditional Chinese glyphs

The 「の」 and 「漢」 icon outlines use [Noto Sans CJK TC Bold](https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk/tree/main/Sans/OTF/TraditionalChinese).
The glyphs were converted to SVG paths and scaled to the Virgo icon grid.
Small chamfers were added to selected outside corners of the Traditional Chinese glyph.
The source font is licensed under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk/blob/main/Sans/LICENSE).
